How this page reads your sheet

It matches columns by their headings: Division, Corps, Team, a contestant-count column, and contestant name and photo columns (either "Contestant 1 Name / Photo 1…" across one row, or one row per contestant). Anything it can't find is skipped. Change the settings at the top of the script to set the team total, allowed team size, or a published Apps Script URL.
